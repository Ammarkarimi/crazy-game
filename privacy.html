Privacy Policy

HYPERCHAOS: Super Silly Space Adventure · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version: HYPERCHAOS does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game saves

Your progress (gems, stickers, unlocked rockets, high scores and settings) is saved only on your own device, using your browser's local storage or the desktop app's local data folder. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Erase all progress, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What we don't do

  • No sign-ups, names, emails, photos or locations are asked for
  • No advertising or advertising IDs
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party cookies
  • No way to talk to other players
  • No purchases inside the game

Children

HYPERCHAOS is made for children aged 4 and up. Because the game collects no personal information at all, it is designed to comply with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR-K (EU/UK).

Website hosting

When you play in a web browser, the hosting provider that delivers the game files may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track players.

Cheering voice

The optional cheering voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and the game never uses the microphone or camera.
