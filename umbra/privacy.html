Privacy Policy

UMBRA: Duel of Shadows · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version: UMBRA does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game saves

Your progress (coins, gems, gear, unlocked fighters, honors and settings) is saved only on your own device, using your browser's local storage or the desktop app's local data folder. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Erase all progress, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What we don't do

  • No sign-ups, names, emails, photos or locations are asked for
  • No advertising or advertising IDs
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party cookies
  • No way to talk to other players
  • No purchases inside the game

Children

UMBRA collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it is designed to comply with privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR (EU/UK).

Website hosting

When you play in a web browser, the hosting provider that delivers the game files may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track players.

Announcer voice

The optional announcer uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and the game never uses the microphone or camera.
