HOW TO PLAY

SURVIVE

Clear 20 waves of neon horrors. Every 5th wave a boss arrives.

Collect XP crystals to level up and pick upgrades. Stack them into ridiculous builds.

Kills charge your CHAOS meter. When full, unleash OVERDRIVE: invincibility, triple fire rate and slowed time.

From wave 2, CHAOS EVENTS can twist the rules: meteor storms, mirror worlds, black holes and more.

Spend shards on permanent upgrades and new ships.
