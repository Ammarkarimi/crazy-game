HOW TO PLAY

🚀
Fly your rocket! It shoots sparkle bubbles all by itself.
😠
The Grumpies are grumpy. Hit them with bubbles to cheer them up! 😊
⭐
Grab the stars to level up and pick a new super power!
🌈
Fill the rainbow bar, then press the rainbow button for RAINBOW MODE!
💎
Collect gems to buy new rockets and powers in the shop.
❤️
Don't bump into Grumpies or their bubbles! Grab hearts to heal.
